Profile

Systems leader. Public health architect.

I am a public health systems architect and program management strategist with deep expertise in building, scaling, and sustaining the infrastructure that makes public health work. As Performance Improvement Director at the Kansas Department of Health and Environment, I direct post-award administration of more than $64M in federally funded public health grants — including the CDC Public Health Infrastructure Grant and the COVID-19 Community Health Initiative.

My work spans performance management system design, CLAS implementation, language access infrastructure, workforce planning, and multi-funder grant compliance. I design the systems, build the governance structures, and deliver the evidence that makes public health investment defensible — and durable.

Outside of KDHE, I serve as a Public Health Foundation consultant, run an independent consulting practice, and hold civic appointments including Congressional District 2 Commissioner on the Kansas African American Affairs Commission.
